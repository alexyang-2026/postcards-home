Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Postcards Home. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Uploaded photographs
  • Postcard captions
  • Life Segments
  • Collectibles and inventory progress
  • Wallpaper and personalization preferences
  • Location (only if you explicitly grant permission)
  • Dates associated with postcards

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to operate the application, including:

  • Saving your postcards.
  • Keeping your account synchronized across devices.
  • Displaying your inventory and collectibles.
  • Providing personalized features.
  • Improving the application.

4. Location Data

Location is optional. If you choose to grant location permission, it is used only to display locations on postcards and to unlock location-based collectibles.

5. Data Storage

Account information and uploaded content are securely stored using Supabase cloud services.

6. Third-Party Services

Postcards Home currently uses third-party services including:

  • Supabase (authentication and cloud storage)
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Postcards Home uses essential browser storage to keep you signed in and remember application settings.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is only shared with third-party services when necessary to provide the application's functionality.

9. Your Rights

You may:

  • Update your account information.
  • Delete your account.
  • Request deletion of your stored data.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated sporadically. The latest version will always be available on this page.
