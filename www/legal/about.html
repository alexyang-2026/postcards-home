Our Mission

Modern phones contain thousands of photographs, yet many of our memories gradually lose their context over time.

Postcards Home was created to encourage people to preserve meaningful moments rather than simply collecting pictures.

Collect Memories Through Multiple Senses

Every postcard combines a photograph, caption, location, music, and personal touches into something worth revisiting years later.

As a musician myself...

The collectible system is designed to encourage exploration, reflection, and creativity.

Whether you unlock a peaceful wallpaper, discover an exclusive piece of music, or complete a memorable Life Segment, every collectible tells part of your story.

Created By Alex Yang

Thank you for trying Postcards Home. I hope it helps you preserve memories that might otherwise have been forgotten.
